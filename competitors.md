# Competitor Research — Madrid (Small/Medium Obra, Reformas, Excavación, Transporte)

Goal: Build a site that beats typical Madrid competitors on:
- clarity of services (structured, not “hacemos de todo” chaos)
- modern industrial visual style
- WhatsApp-first conversion (sticky CTA + quote form → WhatsApp)
- trust signals (proof, process, real photos)

## Competitor List (URLs + Quick Critique)

### 1) Consolidaciones y Contratas
URL: https://www.consolidacionesycontratas.com/
What they do well:
- Trust framing (experience / “llave en mano” tone)
What to beat:
- Traditional look; likely not WhatsApp-first
- Service structure could be clearer and more visual

Steal:
- “llave en mano + plazos” style trust language (rewrite cleaner)

---

### 2) Espacios Reforver
URL: https://espaciosreforver.com/
What they do well:
- Trust copy: years, seriousness, deadlines
What to beat:
- Reforms-centric; not strong on excavation/transport differentiation
- Typical template vibe

Steal:
- Compact trust bullets (“+años”, “plazos”, “equipo”)

---

### 3) REMCA Obra Civil
URL: https://remcaobracivil.com/
What they do well:
- Simple “construction in Madrid” framing
What to beat:
- Generic/corporate feel
- Not targeted to small/medium + direct WhatsApp conversion

Steal:
- Clean navigation structure, but make it warmer and more direct

---

### 4) Excavaciones Paniagua
URL: https://empresaexcavacionesmadrid.com/
What they do well:
- Direct service list (movimiento de tierras, demoliciones, etc.)
- “visita previa” / personal attention framing
What to beat:
- UI/UX can be cleaner; project proof can be stronger
- Make WhatsApp the primary funnel

Steal:
- “visita sin compromiso” + “atención personalizada”

---

### 5) Tranide
URL: https://tranide.com/
What they do well:
- Clear service taxonomy (excavación / transporte / alquiler)
What to beat:

- Less “trato directo” for small/medium clients

- Service breakdown and machinery credibility language

---

### 6) Reforfast

What to beat:

---

### 7) Materiales Construcción OLIMA (Adjacent)
URL: https://materialesconstruccionolima.com/

## Market Patterns (Common Weaknesses to Exploit)
- Generic copy (“hacemos todo”) without structure
- Too much text, weak hierarchy
- Weak mobile UX and slow pages
- WhatsApp hidden or absent
- Trust proof missing (real photos, process, address, guarantees)

## Winning Strategy for Richard’s Site
- Positioning: pequeña empresa estructurada + trato directo
- Services organized into 4 blocks:
  1) Excavación y movimiento de tierras
  2) Construcción y reformas (vivienda + industrial)
  3) Transporte de materiales
  4) Trabajos generales de obra (demoliciones, desescombros, preparación)
- WhatsApp-first conversion:
  - sticky WhatsApp button
  - primary CTA above the fold
  - “contact form” builds message + opens WhatsApp
- Proof-first: years, plazos, visita sin compromiso, zonas Madrid
- Mixed imagery: stock for hero/services; real photos for projects only
